What TeaGo Offers

Everything in One Platform

From daily deliveries to monthly financial statements — TeaGo handles every step of your tea factory's operations.

🍃

Tea Delivery Tracking

Record daily tea leaf deliveries with land owner details and weight. Instant dashboard updates for full visibility.

💰

Payment Calculations

Payments are automatically calculated based on tea weight and configured rate per kg — with a saved rate for each delivery.

🌿

Fertilizer Management

Land owners request fertilizers; factory owners approve and auto-deduct costs from monthly billing.

📋

Loan & Advance System

Structured loan application, approval workflow, and automatic cheque generation with factory-managed delivery.

📊

Monthly Bills & Reports

Generate detailed monthly bills and income statements (3 or 6 months) for transparency and record-keeping.

👷

Employee Management

Manage worker records, track work days, and auto-calculate salaries — all within the platform.

🔒

Secure Authentication

Role-based access control ensures that land owners and factory owners see only what they need.

📣

Complaint Management

Land owners can submit complaints directly to the factory. Factories respond within the system seamlessly.

How TeaGo Works

Get up and running in minutes. Our streamlined process eliminates paperwork and manual errors.

01

Register

Sign up as a land owner or factory owner with your NIC and contact details.

02

Record Deliveries

Factory owners record tea deliveries; the system calculates earnings from the recorded weight and rate.

03

Manage Requests

Submit fertilizer or loan requests. Factory owners review and approve within the platform.

04

Generate Reports

At month end, generate bills and income statements with a single click — ready to download.
